Polynomials & Rational Expressions

Master this unit to unlock the concepts that follow

📖 Unit Overview

Polynomials are sums of terms with variables raised to whole-number powers. From simple binomials to complex multivariate expressions, polynomials are the workhorses of algebra. You'll factor them, divide them, and use them to model everything from projectile motion to profit functions.

Real-World Context: Polynomial models appear throughout science and economics. Revenue = Price × Quantity, or motion equations like height = −16t² + 64t + 5. Factoring skills unlock solving polynomial equations.

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this unit, you'll be able to:

  1. Add, subtract, and multiply polynomials
  2. Factor polynomials using various techniques (GCF, grouping, trinomial patterns, special products)
  3. Divide polynomials using long division and synthetic division
  4. Simplify rational expressions and identify restrictions
  5. Solve polynomial and rational equations
