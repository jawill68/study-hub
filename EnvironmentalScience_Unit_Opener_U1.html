📚 Ecology & Ecosystems

Explore how organisms interact with each other and their physical environment. This unit builds the foundation for understanding energy flow, population dynamics, and community structure—the building blocks of all ecological systems.

From a single cell to entire rainforests, every living system operates on principles of energy transfer and resource exchange. Understanding ecology helps us explain why deserts support fewer species than rainforests, why food chains rarely exceed five levels, and how removing one species can collapse an entire ecosystem.

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Explain how biotic and abiotic factors interact to define an ecosystem
  • Trace energy flow through food chains and food webs using the 10% rule
  • Analyze how carrying capacity and limiting factors regulate population growth
  • Compare primary and secondary succession and predict ecosystem development
  • Model the effect of removing a species on ecosystem structure and function

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid:
  • m1: Ecology vs Environmentalism
  • m2: Biotic vs Abiotic Factors
  • m3: Energy Loss in Food Chains
  • m4: Only Plants Are Producers
  • m5: Succession Always Increases Biodiversity
  • m6: Carrying Capacity Is Fixed
  • m7: Limiting Factors Are Most Abundant
  • m8: Succession Requires Bare Rock
  • m9: Photosynthesis Only Happens in Sunlight
  • m10: All Density-Dependent Factors Are Biotic
