📖 Unit Overview

Quadratics are degree-2 polynomials that graph as parabolas. Their symmetry and well-understood properties make them ideal for modeling motion, profit optimization, and design problems. You'll solve them, graph them, and apply them to real situations.

Real-World Context: A ball thrown upward follows a quadratic path. Profit functions are often quadratic. Design problems—building bridges, designing antennas—rely on parabolic shapes. Understanding quadratics is critical for STEM fields.

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this unit, you'll be able to:

  1. Solve quadratic equations by factoring, completing the square, and the quadratic formula
  2. Graph parabolas and identify key features (vertex, axis of symmetry, intercepts)
  3. Apply quadratics to optimization and motion problems
  4. Work with complex numbers when discriminant is negative
