Recommended study path
1
Learn the words
One unit at a time in the Glossary. Run Flip Cards daily for 10 minutes; star what you miss.
2
Drill in Practice mode
Read every explanation, even on questions you got right. Ask TEAL when something doesn't click.
3
Untangle the look-alikes and autopsy your errors
Run Speed Sort until you hit 90%. Mark autopsies mastered only when you could teach them.
4
Write it out
Do two Self-Grader prompts a week on paper. CER writing is where honors grades are won.
5
Climb the certification track
After Unit 5, start DU domain drills and the Field Guide. Take a Quick 25 every two weeks; aim for 80%+ readiness before your exam date.
6
Test under pressure
Before unit tests, run Exam mode. Use Weak Spots mode to clear out everything you've missed.
