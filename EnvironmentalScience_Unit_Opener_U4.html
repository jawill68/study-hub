📚 Environmental Quality

Examine the impacts of human activity on air, water, and soil quality. This unit explores pollution pathways, bioaccumulation, climate change, and solutions—from personal choices to global agreements.

Atmospheric CO₂ has risen 50% since the industrial revolution. Dead zones cover 130,000 km² of ocean. But renewable energy is the fastest-growing energy source, and the Montreal Protocol has begun healing the ozone hole. This unit connects human behavior to environmental consequences and explores solutions at every scale.

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Trace pollution pathways from source to human exposure and predict health impacts
  • Analyze how bioaccumulation and biomagnification concentrate toxins in food chains
  • Distinguish between ozone depletion and global warming and evaluate solutions
  • Design a mitigation and adaptation strategy for climate change impacts
  • Calculate carbon footprints and evaluate renewable vs nonrenewable energy tradeoffs
