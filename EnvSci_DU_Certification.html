Ducks Unlimited Ecology Conservation & Management Certification

Earn an industry certification while you're in high school.

The Ducks Unlimited Ecology Conservation & Management Certification is a 100-question exam on the iCEV Testing Platform. It's on Florida's CAPE industry certification funding list, so passing earns acceleration points. Everything here is original practice organized by the exam's four weighted standards. Use it alongside your class curriculum, not instead of it.
