21 Term Pairs Students Most Often Confuse

The Essential 50 and Complete Reference define terms in isolation; this resource shows them in contrast. Reading confused terms separately rarely fixes the confusion — students need to see them together. These 21 pairs cover the distinctions where General Paper precision is most often lost, with side-by-side comparisons, memory devices, and teaching notes.

How to Use This Resource

This is a teaching and revision tool, not a glossary. The Essential 50 defines each term individually; this document shows them in contrast. The pairs gathered here are not random — they're the distinctions Cambridge examiners most often see students miss.

Recommended approach

For revision: Read both sides of each confusion. Then close the document and explain the difference out loud in one sentence. If you can't, re-read the "What separates them" and "Memory device" boxes.
For teaching: Project one confusion per lesson opener. Ask students to write the difference before showing the answer. The contrast makes the distinction stick in ways isolated definitions don't.
