Recommended Study Path
1
Build vocabulary first
Start in the Master Glossary. Work one unit at a time and star the terms you find hardest. The vocabulary is the foundation everything else builds on.
2
Drill in Practice mode
Move to the Drill Trainer in Practice mode, one unit at a time. Read every explanation — even on questions you get right — to lock in the reasoning.
3
Sort out the look-alikes
Skim Common Confusions once you've hit a few mix-ups in drilling — mass vs. weight, fission vs. fusion, and similar pairs are worth locking in early rather than re-confusing on exam day.
4
Autopsy your errors
When you start missing questions, open the Mistakes Autopsy. Find the misconception behind each error and mark it mastered once it clicks.
5
Practice writing it out
Work through the Self-Grader's calculation and short-answer prompts on paper. This is the closest thing to a free-response exam question — multiple choice alone won't prepare you for showing your work.
6
Test under exam conditions
Return to the Drill Trainer and run a timed Exam Mode session across all units. Use the per-unit breakdown to see exactly where to circle back.
