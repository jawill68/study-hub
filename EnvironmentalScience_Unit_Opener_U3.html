📚 Sustaining Biodiversity

Learn the strategies, policies, and tools used to protect biodiversity at local and global scales. This unit moves from understanding biodiversity to taking action—through protected areas, captive breeding, restoration, and international agreements.

Extinction rates are 100-1000 times higher than natural background rates. Every species loss is irreversible. But conservation is working: the Arabian oryx was extinct in the wild and has been reintroduced through captive breeding. Giant pandas have recovered from 1,000 to 1,800 individuals. This unit explores how humans can reverse biodiversity loss through science, policy, and commitment.

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Evaluate the effectiveness of different conservation strategies (in situ vs ex situ)
  • Design a comprehensive conservation plan for an endangered species
  • Analyze the role of protected areas in maintaining ecosystem functions
  • Assess the benefits and risks of ecotourism as a conservation tool
  • Recommend international policy solutions for biodiversity loss
