Systems, Matrices & Probability

Master this unit to unlock the concepts that follow

📖 Unit Overview

Systems of equations model situations with multiple constraints. Matrices organize and solve systems efficiently. Probability and statistics quantify uncertainty and variability. Together, these tools handle complex, real-world scenarios.

Real-World Context: Supply-and-demand equilibrium (business), nutrition planning (diet systems), electrical circuits (Kirchhoff's laws), sports analytics (win probabilities), medical screening (conditional probability), data analysis.

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this unit, you'll be able to:

  1. Solve systems of equations using elimination, substitution, and matrices
  2. Perform matrix operations and interpret results
  3. Calculate probabilities and conditional probabilities
  4. Work with normal distributions and standard deviation
  5. Apply statistical thinking to data and claims
