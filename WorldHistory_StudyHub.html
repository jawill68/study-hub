Recommended Study Path
1
Build vocabulary by era
Start in the Master Glossary. Work one unit at a time in chronological order — Geography, then Early Modern, Modern, and Contemporary — and read the examples closely; they anchor each term to a specific event.
2
Drill in Practice mode
Move to the Drill Trainer in Practice mode, unit by unit. Read every explanation — many questions test cause-and-effect relationships, not just recall.
3
Untangle the mix-ups
Open the Mistakes Autopsy and study the errors that recur most on assessments — confused empires, reversed chronology, cause/effect swaps. Mark each one mastered once it clicks.
4
Test under exam conditions
Return to the Drill Trainer and run a timed Exam Mode session across all units. Use the per-unit breakdown to target your remaining review time.
